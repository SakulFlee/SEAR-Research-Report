\abstract
This research report will evaluate rather, Dependency-Injection in Java opens more potential risks and threads to the application, or if it is actually worth the risk and comes with many benefits.
Additionally, some methods to prevent such attacks and avoid any dangers are analyzed.

This report is written by Lukas Weber and Gianluca Biancolin as part of the SEAR\footnote{Security and Research} class from the FHTenL\footnote{Fontys University of Applied Science / Technic and Logistic, Venlo, the Netherlands} and will be graded.
